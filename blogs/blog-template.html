Statutory Rule: Regulation 22 Parity Clause

Under UGC Open and Distance Learning and Online Programmes Regulations 2020, degrees conferred by recognized institutions in online mode are treated as 100% equivalent to degrees awarded on conventional university campuses for all jobs and higher studies.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the statutory validity of this degree?

Degrees awarded by UGC-DEB recognized universities carry 100% statutory equivalence under UGC Regulation 22. They are fully recognized for UPSC, SSC, Banking, state PSC exams, and global credential evaluations like WES.

2. Can I pursue this program while working full-time?

Yes. Online and Distance MBA programs are designed specifically for working professionals, featuring self-paced recorded lectures, evening live interactive webinars, and weekend doubt clearing sessions.

3. Are 0% No-Cost EMI payment plans available?

Yes. Accredited universities offer 0% interest No-Cost EMI options spread over 12, 18, or 24 monthly installments with zero down payment and zero hidden processing charges.

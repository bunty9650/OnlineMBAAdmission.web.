3. 4-Semester 102-Credit Academic Curriculum

The 102-credit curriculum is structured across 4 semesters (where 1 credit represents 30 hours of learner engagement) using the UGC 4-quadrant SWAYAM framework:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations - 27 Credits)

• Principles of Management and Organizational Behavior (4 Credits)
• Marketing Management (4 Credits)
• Accounting for Managers (4 Credits)
• Business Economics (4 Credits)
• Business Communication (4 Credits)
• Legal Aspects of Business (4 Credits)
• Computer Application in Management (3 Credits)

Semester 2 (Analytical Core - 27 Credits)

• Quantitative Methods in Management (4 Credits)
• Human Resource Management (4 Credits)
• Financial Management (4 Credits)
• Productions and Operations Management (4 Credits)
• Research Methodology and Statistical Techniques (4 Credits)
• Global Economic Environment and Policy (4 Credits)
• Management Information Systems (3 Credits)

Semester 3 (Core and Specialization - 24 Credits)

• Project Management (Core - 4 Credits)
• Business Strategy (Core - 4 Credits)
• Human Resource Management Electives: Industrial Relation, Competency Mapping and Performance Management (8 Credits)
• Second Chosen Dual Specialization Electives (8 Credits)

Semester 4 (Advanced Core and Project - 24 Credits)

• Banking and Insurance Management (Core - 4 Credits)
• Human Resource Management Electives: Organization Development and Human Resource Development, Compensation Management and International Human Resource Management (8 Credits)
• Second Chosen Dual Specialization Electives (8 Credits)
• Project Work / Capstone Thesis (Core - 4 Credits)

4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the Sikkim Manipal University Online MBA in Human Resource Management is open to candidates meeting statutory graduation norms:

Undergraduate Degree Requirement

Candidates must hold a 10+2+3, 10+2+4, or 10+3+3 bachelor degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Direct Merit-Based Admission

Admissions are conducted through verified academic records and merit without the mandatory requirement of CAT, MAT, or XAT scores. Working professionals can enroll directly online.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the Online MBA in Human Resource Management from SMU UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. Sikkim Manipal University is fully entitled by the UGC-DEB and accredited with NAAC Grade A+. Under UGC regulations, online degrees have identical legal validity to traditional on-campus degrees.

How are examinations conducted for SMU online students? ▾

Examinations are conducted 100% online through an AI-proctored digital evaluation system. You can take your semester examinations conveniently from home without visiting a test center.

How does Coursera integration benefit SMU MBA learners? ▾

SMU MBA students receive complimentary access to global certifications from industry leaders and international institutions hosted on Coursera, providing practical credentials alongside their master degree.

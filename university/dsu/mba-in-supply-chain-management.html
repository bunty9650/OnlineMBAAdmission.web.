01. Executive Overview and Strategic Value in 2027

The Online MBA in Supply Chain Management at Dayananda Sagar University (DSU Online), Bengaluru, is designed for forward-looking professionals aiming to master contemporary leadership practices within end-to-end procurement, multimodal logistics, and supply network resilience. Established by the Karnataka State Act in 2014 and rooted in a seven-decade educational heritage dating back to the 1960s, Dayananda Sagar University combines rigorous academic heritage with Bangalore's dynamic technological and commercial ecosystem.

In the rapidly shifting corporate environment of 2027, management leaders require a multi-disciplinary synthesis of conceptual foundations, applied quantitative frameworks, and executive decision-making capabilities. DSU Online's curriculum incorporates experiential learning, case-study methodologies, live interactive masterclasses, and an innovative dual specialization architecture that allows learners to combine 1 major domain with 1 complementary minor domain across management disciplines.

Statutory Equality Under UGC Regulation 22 (2020)

Degrees awarded under Dayananda Sagar University online degree programs hold statutory recognition across all Central and State Government examinations, public sector recruitment boards, multinational corporate recruitments, and higher academic credentials including Ph.D. registrations and national eligibility tests.

Through a modern four-quadrant learning management system, asynchronous learning materials, and dedicated student advisory teams, students build executive competence without sacrificing their career trajectory or personal commitments.

05. Digital LMS Architecture, Faculty Mentors and Learning Framework

Dayananda Sagar University's state-of-the-art e-learning infrastructure delivers a structured, four-quadrant pedagogical model that balances self-paced flexibility with active academic interaction:

  • Quadrant I (e-Tutorials): High-definition video and audio lectures delivered by distinguished senior professors and seasoned corporate executives.
  • Quadrant II (e-Content): Downloadable digital course packs, structured reading materials, case study compendiums, and digital library e-books accessible 24/7.
  • Quadrant III (Web Resources and Discussion Forums): Interactive peer discussion channels, live doubt-clearing sessions, and collaborative academic forums monitored by teaching assistants.
  • Quadrant IV (Assessment): Formative weekly quizzes, continuous internal assessment assignments, practical case analyses, and self-evaluation tests.
  • 100% Proctored Online Examinations: Term-end evaluations conducted securely through an AI-enabled remote proctoring system with zero mandatory physical campus visits.

06. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Digital Admission Guide

Minimum Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline from a university recognized by UGC or AIU.
  • A minimum aggregate score of 50% marks in the qualifying undergraduate degree (45% for candidates belonging to SC, ST, or OBC categories).
  • No national entrance examination (such as CAT, MAT, or CMAT) is required for direct admission under UGC-DEB distance and online education regulations.
  • Working professionals, corporate executives, and fresh graduates meeting academic criteria are eligible to apply directly.

07. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the DSU Online MBA in Supply Chain Management recognized by UGC?

Yes. Dayananda Sagar University is entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) and holds NAAC Grade A+ accreditation. Degrees are fully equivalent to conventional on-campus degrees under UGC Regulation 22 (2020).

Can I select both a major and minor specialization in DSU Online MBA?

Yes. DSU Online offers a dual elective model allowing students to pursue 1 major domain such as Supply Chain Management and 1 complementary minor domain to broaden corporate leadership versatility.

How are term-end examinations conducted?

All term-end examinations are administered 100% online through an AI-proctored remote testing portal, allowing working professionals to take exams securely from home.

What are the career placement services provided by DSU?

Students gain access to DSU's dedicated placement cell with 700+ corporate hiring partners, virtual job fairs, resume optimization, and executive interview preparation workshops.

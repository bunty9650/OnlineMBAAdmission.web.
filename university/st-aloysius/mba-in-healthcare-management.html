01. Specialization Overview & Market Value

Students learn not only classical leadership, management economics, and corporate governance but also how algorithmic models and generative tools streamline day-to-day operations and empower predictive corporate planning.

02. Institutional Standing & Statutory Validity

Founded in 1880 by the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) in Mangaluru, Karnataka, St. Aloysius possesses an illustrious 146-year academic heritage. In January 2024, the Ministry of Education and University Grants Commission (UGC) conferred Deemed-to-be University status upon the institution alongside prestigious UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy.

UGC Gazette Notification Parity: Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, master's degrees awarded through UGC-entitled online mode hold 100% legal equivalence with traditional conventional on-campus degrees. They are completely valid for UPSC Civil Services, State Public Service Commissions (PSCs), Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), higher doctoral studies (Ph.D.), and global credential assessments (WES Canada & USA).

With an outstanding NAAC Grade A++ accreditation (CGPA 3.67 out of 4.00), St. Aloysius ranks among the highest accredited institutions in India, assuring prospective students and recruiters of stringent curriculum rigor, faculty excellence, and uncompromised examination integrity.

05. Eligibility Criteria & Direct Admission Guidelines

In accordance with UGC-DEB statutory guidelines, admission into the St. Aloysius Online MBA in Healthcare Management & Applied AI is merit-based with zero entrance exam barrier:

Academic Qualification

Bachelor's degree in any discipline (B.Com, BBA, B.Sc, B.A., B.Tech, etc.) from a UGC-recognized university.

Minimum Aggregate Marks

Minimum 50% aggregate marks in graduation for General category candidates (45% for SC/ST/OBC categories).

Entrance Exams (CAT / MAT)

Zero entrance scorecard needed. Admission is evaluated purely on graduation academic merit.

* Working executives, fresh graduates, entrepreneurs, and defense personnel are all equally eligible to apply.

08. Frequently Asked Questions (St. Aloysius Online MBA)

Verified, official answers to the most common queries regarding this specialization:

Who is eligible for Online MBA in Healthcare Management? +

Graduates from any stream are eligible, including MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, B.Pharm, B.Sc Nursing, Biotechnology, and general graduates (B.Com/BBA/B.Sc/B.Tech) seeking hospital management careers with minimum 50% marks.

What is the statutory recognition of this degree? +

Conferred by St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University) under UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy with NAAC A++ rating, the degree holds 100% legal parity with on-campus master's degrees under UGC Regulation 22.

What are the total tuition fees and installment options? +

Standard Track tuition is ₹80,000 total (₹22,000/sem), and the Applied AI Track is ₹1,20,000 total (₹33,000/sem). No-cost EMI plans start from ₹3,333/month.

Does the course teach Indian healthcare standards like ABDM and NABH? +

Yes. The curriculum specifically incorporates NABH accreditation protocols and hands-on exposure to Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) and FHIR interoperability standards.

Are examinations conducted online from home? +

Yes. 100% of term-end examinations are conducted remotely online with AI webcam proctoring without center visits.

Can I manage this course while working in hospital duty shifts? +

Yes. With weekend live webinars and 24/7 cloud LMS access, doctors, nursing supervisors, and healthcare executives can review recorded lectures according to their shift schedules.

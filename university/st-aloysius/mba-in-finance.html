01. Specialization Overview & Market Value

The Online MBA in Financial Management & Applied AI at St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University) is specifically calibrated to address the primary demand of today's executive employment market: the union of strategic business acumen with practical, data-driven automation.

Rather than treating modern technology as an abstract elective, St. Aloysius embeds domain-specific intelligence directly into foundational and specialized business subjects. Students learn not only classical leadership, management economics, and corporate governance but also how algorithmic models and generative tools streamline day-to-day operations and empower predictive corporate planning.

02. Institutional Standing & Statutory Validity

Founded in 1880 by the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) in Mangaluru, Karnataka, St. Aloysius possesses an illustrious 146-year academic heritage. In January 2024, the Ministry of Education and University Grants Commission (UGC) conferred Deemed-to-be University status upon the institution alongside prestigious UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy.

UGC Gazette Notification Parity: Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, master's degrees awarded through UGC-entitled online mode hold 100% legal equivalence with traditional conventional on-campus degrees. They are completely valid for UPSC Civil Services, State Public Service Commissions (PSCs), Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), higher doctoral studies (Ph.D.), and global credential assessments (WES Canada & USA).

With an outstanding NAAC Grade A++ accreditation (CGPA 3.67 out of 4.00), St. Aloysius ranks among the highest accredited institutions in India, assuring prospective students and recruiters of stringent curriculum rigor, faculty excellence, and uncompromised examination integrity.

09. Frequently Asked Questions (St. Aloysius Online MBA)

Verified, official answers to the most common queries regarding this specialization:

Is the St. Aloysius Online MBA in Finance equivalent to on-campus degrees? +

Yes. Conferred by St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University) under UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy and NAAC A++ accreditation, the degree has 100% statutory equivalence to full-time campus programs under UGC Gazette Regulation 22.

Does the curriculum include practical financial software tools? +

Yes. Students receive hands-on training on Excel with Copilot, Power BI financial dashboards, SAP S/4HANA Finance simulations, Python for financial data, and Screener.in fundamental analysis.

Can Commerce (B.Com / BBA) graduates gain exemptions or credit transfers? +

Graduates from commerce backgrounds benefit from strong foundational alignment in Semesters 1 and 2, but all candidates complete the standard 88 credits to earn the entitled postgraduate degree.

What are the total tuition fees and installment options? +

Standard Track tuition is ₹80,000 total (₹22,000/sem), and the Applied AI Track is ₹1,20,000 total (₹33,000/sem). Interest-free monthly EMIs start from ₹3,333/month.

Are examinations online? +

Yes. All semester examinations are conducted 100% online from home via AI and webcam proctoring software with zero travel requirements.

Is CAT/MAT scorecard mandatory? +

No. Direct merit-based admission is offered based on graduation aggregate marks (minimum 50% for General, 45% for Reserved categories).

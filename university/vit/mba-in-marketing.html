06. Eligibility Criteria and Admission Roadmap 2027

Admission to the Online MBA in Marketing follows a merit-based evaluation of undergraduate credentials in accordance with statutory guidelines:

Academic Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must hold a Bachelor degree of minimum 3 years duration in any discipline from a recognized University or Institution.
  • Minimum aggregate score of 50% in the qualifying undergraduate degree (45% for candidates belonging to SC/ST categories).
  • No CAT, MAT, or state entrance examination score is mandatory; admissions are conducted directly on undergraduate academic merit.
  • Working professionals and corporate-sponsored executives receive preferential batch scheduling.

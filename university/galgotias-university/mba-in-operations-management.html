3. 4-Semester 80-Credit Academic Curriculum

The curriculum requires 80 minimum credits structured across core managerial foundations, specialized Operations Management coursework, and an applied capstone thesis:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations)

• Marketing and Growth Management
• Accounting for Managers
• Managing Self, Teams and Organizations
• Managerial Statistics
• Legal Aspects of Business
• Managerial Economics
• Written Analysis and Communication-I
• Understanding the Firm

Semester 2 (Analytical Core and AI)

• Business Analytics for Managers
• Corporate Finance
• Business Research Methods
• Operations and Process Management
• Innovation and Commercialisation
• Corporate Governance, Ethics, ESG and Sustainability
• AI and Frontier Technologies for Business

Semester 3 (Strategy and Specialization Electives)

• Strategic Management (Core)
• Data Visualisation and Storytelling (Core)
• Operations Management Electives: Global and Resilient Supply Chain Management; Lean, Quality and Continuous Improvement; Operations Research and Analytics; Sustainable Operations and Supply Chain
• Second Chosen Dual Major Elective
• Summer Internship / Live Industry Project

Semester 4 (Advanced Electives and Capstone)

• Operations Management Electives: Project Management; Service Operations and Customer Experience Management; Operations Strategy and Design; Sourcing and Materials Management
• Second Chosen Dual Major Electives
• Industry Research Capstone Thesis

4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the Galgotias University Online MBA in Operations Management follows straightforward criteria:

Undergraduate Degree Requirement

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Final semester undergraduate students can also apply provisionally.

Direct Digital Enrollment

Admissions are conducted on undergraduate academic merit without the requirement of competitive test scores such as CAT, MAT, or CUET, simplifying the enrollment process.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the Online MBA in Operations Management from Galgotias University UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. Galgotias University holds NAAC Grade A+ accreditation and full UGC-DEB entitlement. The online degree holds equal statutory recognition to traditional campus degrees.

How are examinations conducted? ▾

Assessments are administered 100% online through proctored digital platforms, allowing candidates to complete examinations from home.

Can I manage this course while working full-time? ▾

Yes. The program is specifically built for working professionals with 24/7 LMS access, live interactive lectures, recorded archives, and flexible study schedules.

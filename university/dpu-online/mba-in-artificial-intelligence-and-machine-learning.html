3. Semester-wise Detailed Curriculum (102 Credits)

The curriculum features 102 credits across 4 semesters. Semesters 1 and 2 build core management breadth, while Semesters 3 and 4 deliver specialized track mastery:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations - 27 Credits)

• Principles and Practice of Management
• Organizational Behaviour

• Managerial Economics

• Research Methodology

Semester 2 (Applied Management - 27 Credits)

• Human Resource Management
• Marketing Management
• Financial Management
• Operations Management
• Management Information Systems
• Strategic Management and Business Policy
• Entrepreneurship Development

Semester 3 (Specialization Core - 28 Credits)

• OMBAIML 401: Application of AI in Business
• Generic Elective-II (Soft Skills / Business Ethics and Corporate Governance)
• Advanced Concept Labs and Case Study Analysis
• Practical Workshop Applications

Semester 4 (Advanced Mastery and Capstone - 20 Credits)

• OMBAIML 402: Introduction to Deep Learning
• Capstone Project Work Report (OMBP 405)
• Strategic Decision Modeling
• Comprehensive Evaluation and Viva Voce

4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the Online MBA in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning follows straightforward merit guidelines:

Undergraduate Degree

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category applicants).

No Entrance Exam Required

Admission is conducted directly based on graduation merit. Entrance test scores such as CAT, MAT, or XAT are not mandatory. Prior work experience is also not compulsory.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the Online MBA in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from DPU Online UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. The program is offered by Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth Centre for Online Learning (DPU-COL) with UGC entitlement. Under UGC regulations, this online degree is fully recognized and equivalent to on-campus degrees.

How are examinations conducted for online learners? ▾

Examinations are conducted 100% online through proctored digital examination platforms. Students take their term exams from home without needing to travel to a physical test center.

Can I manage this course while working a full-time job? ▾

Yes. The program is designed specifically for working professionals with 24/7 cloud LMS access, recorded video lecture libraries, and fortnightly live faculty interaction sessions.

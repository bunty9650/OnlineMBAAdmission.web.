1. Specialization Overview and Strategic Value

The Online MBA in Marketing Management from Noida International University (NIU) is an intensive, two-year postgraduate management program structured to develop modern leadership competencies, strategic analytical thinking, and hands-on operational acumen in Marketing Management. Built in alignment with the UGC-DEB framework and National Education Policy (NEP 2020), this 104-credit master's program blends theoretical depth with corporate case studies to prepare executives for fast-evolving industries.

Delivered 100% online through NIU CDOE's modern Learning Management System (LMS), learners master modern frameworks, strategic decision tools, and advanced methodologies without needing to pause their professional careers. Whether you are an aspiring corporate manager, functional executive, or entrepreneur, completing the Online MBA in Marketing Management from NIU establishes you as a certified management professional equipped with end-to-end expertise in Marketing Management.

3. Semester-wise Detailed Curriculum (104 Credits)

The Online MBA in Marketing Management features a comprehensive 104-credit framework divided across four distinct semesters. Semesters 1 and 2 establish core general management capabilities, while Semesters 3 and 4 deliver specialized functional mastery and an executive capstone dissertation:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations - 26 Credits)

• Management Principles and Organizational Behavior
• Managerial Economics and Quantitative Decision Models
• Accounting for Business and Financial Reporting
• Business Environment, Ethics and Corporate Law
• Executive Communication and Leadership Dynamics

Semester 2 (Applied Management - 26 Credits)

• Strategic Financial Management
• Marketing Management and Consumer Analysis
• Human Resource Management and Organizational Culture
• Production and Operations Foundations
• Business Research Methods and Analytical Tools

Semester 3 (Marketing Management Electives - 26 Credits)

• Strategic Management and Corporate Governance
• Consumer Behavior and Neuromarketing Insights (4 Credits)
• Digital and Performance Marketing Strategies (4 Credits)
• Sales Management and Channel Architecture (4 Credits)
• Strategic Brand Management and Equity Positioning (4 Credits)
• Industry Case Studies and Summer Project Internship

Semester 4 (Advanced Functional Mastery - 26 Credits)

• Global Business Strategy and Innovation Leadership
• Integrated Marketing Communications and Public Relations (4 Credits)
• B2B Marketing and Corporate Key Account Management (4 Credits)
• Services Marketing and Customer Experience Design (4 Credits)
• Global Marketing Strategy and Cross-Border Expansion (4 Credits)
• Executive Master's Project Dissertation and Viva Voce

4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission Rules 2027

Admission to the Online MBA in Marketing Management for the 2027 academic session follows straightforward merit-based criteria established under UGC guidelines:

Graduation Requirement

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution. Final semester graduation students are also eligible to apply provisionally.

Work Experience and Entrances

Prior work experience is not strictly mandatory, making the program accessible to fresh graduates as well as seasoned working professionals. No CAT, MAT, or competitive entrance score is required for standard admission cycles.

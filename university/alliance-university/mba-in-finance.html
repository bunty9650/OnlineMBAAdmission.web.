1. Executive Overview and 2027 Industry Landscape

The global financial ecosystem in 2027 is undergoing fundamental restructuring driven by algorithmic trading, decentralized financial architectures, regulatory technology (RegTech), and artificial intelligence. Financial executives and corporate controllers are expected to transcend historical bookkeeping, evolving into strategic navigators who evaluate complex cross-border risks, optimize capital structures, and identify value-accretive corporate mergers. Global corporations, investment banking houses, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), and FinTech institutions require leaders capable of rigorous quantitative analysis alongside strategic risk governance.

The Online MBA in Finance at Alliance University is developed by seasoned financial scholars and senior banking practitioners to deliver rigorous conceptual foundations combined with applied analytical tools. Aligned with global accounting and regulatory standards, the curriculum integrates corporate finance using AI, risk intelligence, venture financing structures, and securities valuation to prepare agile financial executives capable of leading high-stakes capital allocations in international commercial markets.

AACSB-Aligned Quality Benchmarks

Alliance University's business education is governed by AACSB international membership principles, fostering analytical rigor and responsible leadership.

AlliO Digital Learning Companion

Interactive self-paced e-learning modules, continuous automated assessments, and round-the-clock digital library connectivity.

2. Core Competencies Developed in Online MBA in Finance

Throughout the 80-credit academic journey, learners cultivate five foundational pillars of operational and executive competence:

Security Valuation and Portfolio Engineering

Apply capital asset pricing models, discounted cash flow valuations, multi-factor risk frameworks, and quantitative asset allocation strategies across global markets.

Multinational Financial Risk Management

Manage cross-border foreign exchange exposure, sovereign political risks, international interest rate swaps, and multinational capital budgeting decisions.

AI-Enabled Fraud Detection and RegTech

Deploy machine learning anomaly detection models to mitigate payment fraud, ensure anti-money laundering (AML) compliance, and strengthen enterprise credit risk controls.

Venture Capital and Startup Financial Architecture

Design funding rounds, term sheet agreements, valuation waterfalls, convertible debt instruments, and equity dilution models for high-growth enterprises.

Corporate Restructuring and M&A Valuation

Evaluate corporate mergers, hostile takeovers, divestitures, synergy valuations, leveraged buyouts (LBO), and post-merger integration dynamics.

3. 4-Semester Coursework and Detailed Module Breakdown

The academic curriculum is structured into core foundational semesters followed by domain-specialized electives and an individual industry dissertation:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations - 20 Credits)

• Intelligent Economics for Managers
• Accounting for Managerial Decisions
• Marketing Practices in Digital Age
• Organizational Behaviour
• Operations Management
• Business Statistics

Semester 2 (Applied Management - 20 Credits)

• Applied Business Research Methods
• Macroeconomic Environment and Policy
• Human Resource Management
• Corporate Finance using AI
• Strategic Management
• Design Thinking

Semester 3 (Elective Modules - 20 Credits)

  • Security Analysis and Portfolio Management: Covers fundamental and technical equity analysis, efficient market hypothesis, modern portfolio theory, and active portfolio rebalancing.
  • Multinational Financial Management: Examines currency derivatives, foreign exchange risk hedging, international transfer pricing, and cross-border working capital management.
  • AI-Enabled Fraud Detection and Risk Management: Explores predictive credit scoring, fraud prevention machine learning algorithms, transaction monitoring, and financial compliance automation.
  • Modern Financial Ecosystem: Focuses on FinTech platforms, digital payment systems, open banking APIs, peer-to-peer lending models, and central bank digital currencies (CBDC).
  • Financial Architecture for New Ventures: Covers venture capital valuation methods, angel syndicate funding, SAFE notes, capitalization tables, and financial planning for early-stage startups.

Semester 4 (Advanced Electives and Thesis - 20 Credits)

  • Revenue Intelligence for Fixed Income: Covers bond yield curve analytics, duration and convexity modeling, credit default swaps (CDS), and structured fixed-income securities.
  • Mergers, Acquisition and Corporate Restructuring: In-depth valuation of corporate acquisitions, regulatory antitrust compliance, hostile takeover defense, and financial restructuring.
  • Financial Analysis and Modelling: Hands-on financial three-statement model construction, scenario analysis, discounted cash flow (DCF) templates, and Monte Carlo risk simulations.
  • AI and Human Biases in Finance: Investigates behavioral finance phenomena, cognitive heuristics, overconfidence bias, algorithmic trading ethics, and automated decision governance.
  • Dissertation and Viva-Voce (Industry Capstone): Comprehensive research project analyzing real-world domain challenges.

6. Four-Quadrant LMS Model and Examination Framework (70:30 Ratio)

Alliance Online operates under the University Grants Commission's mandated four-quadrant digital education system:

  • Quadrant I (e-Tutorial): High-resolution recorded video masterclasses, animated case studies, and live interactive lectures hosted by Professors of Practice.
  • Quadrant II (e-Content): Downloadable digital course packs, curated academic reading lists, research papers, and self-instructional e-books.
  • Quadrant III (Discussion Forums): Continuous asynchronous discussion boards for peer learning, faculty doubt clearing, and collaborative business case analysis.
  • Quadrant IV (Self-Assessment): Formative weekly quizzes, automated multiple-choice test banks, and guided analytical business assignments.

Summative Evaluation and Passing Ordinance

Student academic evaluation consists of:

  • Semester-End Examination (SEE) - 70% Weightage: Conducted 100% online in a computer-based, remotely proctored environment with automated and live webcam monitoring. Working professionals can sit for examinations from home within designated scheduling windows.
  • During-Semester Assessment (DSA) - 30% Weightage: Ongoing continuous assessment through quizzes, corporate case reports, and LMS learning tasks.
  • Passing Standard: Students must maintain regular LMS engagement and secure a minimum of 50% marks in both the DSA and SEE components to successfully pass each subject.

7. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Merit Admission 2027

Admission to Online MBA in Finance for the 2027 academic intake is conducted via direct digital merit evaluation without requiring mandatory CAT or MAT examination scores:

Statutory Eligibility Requirements

• Candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years duration) in any discipline from a UGC-recognized university.
• General category candidates must have secured a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in their undergraduate degree.
• Reserved category candidates (SC/ST) are entitled to a 5% relaxation, requiring a minimum of 45% aggregate marks.
• Working professionals, executives, and fresh graduates from commerce, engineering, science, or humanities backgrounds are equally eligible.

Digital Application Sequence

  1. Online Registration: Fill out the digital application form on the official Alliance Online admissions portal.
  2. Academic Document Upload: Upload scanned self-attested copies of 10th and 12th certificates, graduation transcripts, degree certificates, and government identification (Aadhaar/Passport).
  3. Enrollment Confirmation: Upon credential verification by the university admissions board, provisional admission is confirmed, followed by student login issuance for the AlliO LMS.

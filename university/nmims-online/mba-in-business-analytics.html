3. 4-Semester 102-Credit Academic Curriculum

The 102-credit curriculum is structured across 4 semesters under the UGC 4-quadrant learning framework:

Semester 1 (Core Foundations - 24 Credits)

• Business Communication (4 Credits)
• Financial Accounting (4 Credits)
• Micro Economics and Macro Economics (4 Credits)
• Organizational Behavior (4 Credits)
• Marketing Management (4 Credits)
• Quantitative Methods – I (4 Credits)

Semester 2 (Analytical Core - 24 Credits)

• Cost and Management Accounting (4 Credits)
• Human Resource Management (4 Credits)
• Strategic Management (4 Credits)
• Business Analytics (4 Credits)
• Legal Aspect of Business (4 Credits)
• Operations Management (4 Credits)

Semester 3 (Core and Electives - 24 Credits)

• Corporate Finance (Core - 4 Credits)
• Research Methodology (Core - 4 Credits)
• Specialization Electives: Data Analytics for Business, Machine Learning for Managers, Business Process Modelling and Management, Visual Analytics (16 Credits)

Semester 4 (Strategy, Project and Electives - 30 Credits)

• Indian Ethos and Ethics (Core - 4 Credits)
• Corporate Sustainability (Core - 4 Credits)
• International Business (Core - 4 Credits)
• Capstone Project (Core - 6 Credits)
• Specialization Electives: Operational and Supply Chain Analytics, Digital Leadership in the Age of Industry 5.0, Consumer Data Analytics (12 Credits)

4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the NMIMS Online MBA in Business Analytics follows clear academic merit requirements:

Undergraduate Degree Requirement

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree (10+2+3 pattern) in any discipline from a recognized University or an equivalent degree recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD category applicants).

Direct Digital Enrollment

Admissions are conducted on undergraduate academic merit without the mandatory requirement of CAT, MAT, or NMAT scores, making it directly accessible for working executives.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the Online MBA in Business Analytics from NMIMS UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. SVKM's NMIMS holds Category 1 Autonomy, NAAC Grade A++ accreditation, and full UGC-DEB entitlement. The online degree holds statutory equality with conventional on-campus master degrees.

How are examinations conducted? ▾

Examinations are administered 100% online through an AI-proctored digital evaluation system, allowing learners to complete assessments from home.

Can working professionals manage this program along with a full-time job? ▾

Yes. The program offers self-paced learning via cloud LMS, 24/7 access to recorded lectures, session transcripts, and mobile learning applications.

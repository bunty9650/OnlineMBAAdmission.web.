1. Executive Overview and 2027 Industry Landscape

Human resource management in 2027 is the central engine of enterprise agility, culture resilience, and organizational performance. Rapid digital transformation, remote-hybrid work arrangements, workforce reskilling imperatives, and rigorous statutory labor codes require HR leaders who understand organizational psychology alongside business strategy.

Bharati Vidyapeeth CDOE delivers a comprehensive 124-credit Master of Business Administration in Human Resource Management. The program covers talent development, labor jurisprudence, industrial relations, performance systems, organizational transformation, and HR analytics, ensuring graduates can lead diverse, global corporate teams with strategic impact and ethical integrity.

Category-1 UGC Autonomy

Conferred Category-1 status by the University Grants Commission, allowing BVDU to maintain contemporary, industry-aligned curricula matching top global standards.

Free Value-Added Certifications

Students receive complimentary skill-enhancement certification programmes alongside the 124-credit degree to sharpen domain-specific corporate readiness.

2. Core Professional Competencies Developed in Online MBA in Human Resource Management

Throughout the 124-credit degree curriculum, learners master five core operational and managerial pillars:

Strategic Workforce Planning and Talent Sourcing

Forecast enterprise staffing requirements, build high-performance talent acquisition channels, and manage executive onboarding journeys.

Performance Management and Total Rewards

Design balanced scorecards, objective key results (OKRs), variable incentive structures, and competitive compensation packages.

Industrial Relations and Labor Law Jurisprudence

Navigate statutory labor legislation, workplace dispute resolution, collective bargaining agreements, and occupational safety regulations.

Organizational Development and Change Management

Direct cultural integration during corporate mergers, lead restructuring initiatives, and foster employee engagement.

HR Metrics, People Analytics and Digital HRIS

Track employee turnover, recruitment velocity, employee net promoter score (eNPS), and human capital return on investment (HC-ROI).

Semester 3 (Specialization and 8-Credit Internship)

  • Strategic Management (Core): Corporate strategies and competitive dynamics.
  • Entrepreneurship Development (Core): New venture creation and business modeling.

  • Summer Internship Project (8 Credits): Mandatory 8-credit corporate HR assignment analyzing talent, compensation, or workforce culture workflows.

Semester 4 (Advanced Electives and Comprehensive Viva)

  • Corporate Governance, Ethics and CSR (Core): Corporate responsibility and stakeholder ethics.
  • Business Analytics and Decision Making (Core): Data-driven executive modeling.
  • Strategic Human Resource Management: Aligning people strategies with business growth goals, international human resources, and workforce restructuring.
  • Organizational Development and Change: Lewin's change model, action research methodologies, organizational culture audits, and intervention design.
  • HR Analytics and Digital HR: Data-driven people modeling, turnover prediction, employee sentiment analysis, and HRIS systems governance.
  • Cross-Cultural and Diversity Management: Inclusion metrics, multicultural workforce integration, expatriate management, and gender equity compliance.
  • Comprehensive Project / Viva-Voce: Concluding master's defense and oral examination.

6. LMS Learning Architecture and Examination Standards

Bharati Vidyapeeth CDOE conducts the program through an advanced, cloud-hosted Learning Management System (LMS):

  • Interactive E-Learning Portal: Live online webinars by experienced faculty, high-definition lecture archives, downloadable digital coursebooks, and interactive discussion forums.
  • Dedicated Academic Mentors: Students are assigned personal faculty mentors to guide them through course subjects, academic doubts, and the 8-credit Summer Internship.
  • Term-End Online Examinations: University examinations are held online at the conclusion of each semester using web and mobile testing interfaces. Students appear remotely with secure digital proctoring.
  • Passing Standard: Students must attain a minimum Grade Point of 5.0 (equivalent to 40% aggregate marks) in each individual course to earn credit.
  • Academic Flexibility: While the regular course duration is 24 months (2 years), learners have up to four academic years from the date of admission to complete all degree requirements.

7. Eligibility Criteria and Digital Admission Process 2027

Admissions for the 2027 academic intake are processed directly on merit without mandatory CAT, MAT, or CMAT examination scores:

Statutory Academic Eligibility Criteria

• Open to any graduate holding a 10+2+3 Bachelor's degree from any recognized university.
• Candidates must have secured a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate at the graduation level.
• Reserved category candidates (SC/ST) are eligible with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate.
• Final year undergraduate students awaiting results may also apply provisionally.

Digital Admission and Maker-Checker Verification Sequence

  1. Online Student Account Creation: Register on the official Bharati Vidyapeeth CDOE portal and complete email validation.
  2. Document Submission: Upload scanned original copies of 10th and 12th mark sheets, graduation certificates, Aadhaar card, ABC ID (Academic Bank of Credits), and passport photograph.
  3. Maker-Checker Scrutiny: The university admissions board verifies submitted credentials and issues provisional admission confirmation via email.
  4. LMS and Mentor Onboarding: Attend the compulsory online academic orientation, access LMS login credentials, and begin scheduled coursework.

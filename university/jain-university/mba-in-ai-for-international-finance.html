4. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the Online MBA in AI for International Finance follows straightforward merit guidelines:

Undergraduate Degree

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years duration) in any discipline from a recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST category applicants). Final semester candidates are also eligible to apply provisionally.

Direct Admission Without CAT

Admission is conducted directly based on undergraduate academic merit. Competitive entrance examination scores such as CAT, MAT, or XAT are not mandatory.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the Online MBA in AI for International Finance from Jain Online UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) holds UGC entitlement for its online degree programs. Under UGC regulations, online degrees carry complete legal equivalence to regular on-campus degrees.

How are examinations conducted for online learners at Jain Online? ▾

Examinations are administered 100% online through proctored digital platforms. Learners take their end-semester examinations from home without physical exam center visits.

Can I manage this course while working full-time? ▾

Yes. The program is specifically tailored for working professionals with weekend live classes, 24/7 cloud LMS access, recorded sessions, and self-paced digital materials.

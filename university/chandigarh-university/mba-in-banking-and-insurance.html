8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this specialization recognized for promotions in nationalized and private banks? ▾

Yes. The degree is UGC-DEB entitled and recognized across public sector banks (SBI, PNB, BoB) and private institutions (HDFC, ICICI, Axis) for officer promotion scales.

Is Online MBA in Banking and Insurance at Chandigarh University recognized by UGC-DEB? ▾

Yes, Chandigarh University Online holds statutory entitlement from the University Grants Commission (UGC) Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB). Under UGC Regulation 22 (2020), this degree is 100% equivalent to conventional on-campus degrees.

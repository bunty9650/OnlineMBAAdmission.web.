Privacy Policy & Data Protection Framework

Transparency, trust, and user privacy are foundational to our advisory services. Read how your enquiry details are protected under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

Status: Draft Framework (Aligned with Statutory Norms — Pending Legal Review) • Last Revised: October 2026

Welcome to Online MBA Admission (accessible at https://www.onlinembaadmission.com). This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling principles, privacy safeguards, and user rights in connection with higher education advisory and academic counseling services.

Regulatory Notice: India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the accompanying MeitY implementation rules follow a phased enforcement schedule. This policy reflects our organizational commitment to data minimization, purpose limitation, and data principal rights in proactive alignment with India's evolving statutory framework, subject to ongoing legal review.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide academic counselling, eligibility verification, fee evaluation, and university recommendations:

  • Personal Identification Details: Full Name.
  • Contact Details: 10-digit Indian Mobile/WhatsApp Number and Email Address.
  • Academic & Professional Preferences: Current educational qualifications (e.g., Graduation percentage, final year status), target MBA specialization (e.g., Finance, Marketing, AI), and preferred university mode (Online MBA or Distance MBA).
  • Technical Browsing Data: Anonymous IP address, browser type, device information, and referring URL collected through privacy-focused web analytics to improve website usability.

2. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and applicable Indian IT Rules, we process your personal information strictly on the basis of your explicit, informed consent. We use your information to:

  • Verify your eligibility for UGC-DEB entitled Online MBA and AICTE approved management programmes.
  • Connect you with qualified academic counsellors via phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your admission enquiry.
  • Provide comparative fee matrices, no-cost EMI options, and admission application assistance.
  • Prevent fraudulent spam submissions and ensure website security.

3. Data Minimization & No Sale of Personal Data

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or commercially monetize your personal data to unauthorized third-party marketing brokers. Your details are strictly utilized for the specific academic counselling request you have initiated.

4. Data Sharing with Educational Processors

To deliver your requested counselling and facilitate direct university interaction, your information may be shared solely with:

  • Accredited University Admissions Departments: Authorized nodal admission representatives of the specific universities you have shortlisted or requested information about.
  • Secure CRM Infrastructure: Enterprise cloud telephony and CRM systems (such as NeoDove) utilized exclusively by our authorized counselling team to organize callback requests securely over encrypted channels (TLS 1.3).

5. Data Security & Storage Standards

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Lead endpoints enforce server-level input sanitization to neutralize malicious payloads and cross-site scripting (XSS).
  • Access to prospective student enquiry details is restricted strictly to authorized academic advisors on a role-based authentication basis.

6. User Consent & Rights

As a data principal, you hold full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke your counselling consent at any time by sending an email with your registered mobile number to admissions@onlinembaadmission.com.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the rectification of inaccurate details or the complete deletion of your enquiry record from our active counselling queue.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Any privacy concerns will be addressed by our designated Grievance Officer within 15 business days.

7. Cookies & Third-Party Analytics

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies and standard Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics scripts to measure page view aggregations and optimize navigation flow. These cookies do not store personally identifiable passwords or financial tokens. You can configure your browser settings to reject cookies without losing access to core website content.

8. Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Advisory Desk

Internal Grievance & Privacy Officer

In accordance with India's applicable Information Technology rules and the statutory grievance redressal principles of the DPDP framework, Online MBA Admission maintains an internal grievance officer to promptly address user concerns regarding data privacy, consent revocation, or communication preferences.

Designation: Internal Grievance Officer (Online MBA Advisory Desk)

Primary Grievance Email: admissions@onlinembaadmission.com

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, statutory regulatory updates from the University Grants Commission (UGC), or changes in privacy legislation. Continued use of the website following published updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

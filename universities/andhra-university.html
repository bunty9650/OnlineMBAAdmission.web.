About Andhra University Online

Andhra University, established in 1926 in the coastal city of Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, is one of India's oldest and most revered State Public Universities. Approaching its historic centenary celebration, Andhra University has educated some of India's most eminent scientists, jurists, civil servants, and corporate leaders.

Andhra University holds an astonishing NAAC Grade A++ with a 3.74 CGPA out of 4.00—one of the highest scores ever awarded to any state government university in India. Through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), the university brings this academic legacy to online learners nationwide.

Operating under full UGC-DEB entitlements and AICTE approval, an Online MBA from Andhra University holds universal legal validity for UPSC Civil Services, state government exams, central PSUs, banking sector jobs, and foreign WES evaluations.

Academic Counselor Verdict: Is Andhra Online Worth It?

Senior Academic Counselor Perspective on Andhra University Online

For students who want the maximum possible NAAC rating (3.74 CGPA) combined with state government university credibility, Andhra University Online is one of India's absolute finest choices. Few universities in India can match its 100-year academic legacy.

As a premier state university, this program delivers unbeatable return on investment. It is the premier recommendation for aspirants seeking complete legal peace of mind for government examinations, PSUs, and international credential evaluation.

  • Highest NAAC A++ (3.74 CGPA): Top 5 among all public state universities in India.
  • Flexible Monthly Installments: Convenient installment options distributed across semesters.
  • 100-Year Heritage (Est. 1926): Unrivaled public credibility and alumni standing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the NAAC rating of Andhra University? ▾
Andhra University holds a prestigious NAAC Grade A++ accreditation with a 3.74 CGPA out of 4.00, which is among the highest scores awarded to any state university in India.
What financial support and installment plans are available for Andhra University Online MBA? ▾
As a state public university, programs are structured with affordable semester installments alongside convenient monthly EMI payment options.
Is Andhra University Online MBA valid for UPSC and government jobs? ▾
Yes, 100%. As a recognized state public university operating under UGC Regulation 22 (2020), its online degrees are completely equivalent to regular campus degrees for UPSC, SSC, Banking, and PSUs.
How are examinations conducted for Andhra University Online? ▾
Examinations are conducted through secure online proctored testing at the end of each semester.
Do I need to qualify AP ICET or CAT for Andhra University Online MBA? ▾
No. Admission to the online MBA program does not require AP ICET or CAT scores; it is based directly on graduation merit (minimum 50% marks).
Is Andhra University degree recognized abroad by WES? ▾
Yes. Andhra University is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and is listed with WES (World Education Services) for Canada and USA credential evaluations.

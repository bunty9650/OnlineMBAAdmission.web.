About Chaudhary Charan Singh University Online (CCSU CDOE)

Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), formerly Meerut University, is a prestigious State Public University established in 1965 in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. Spread across a sprawling 222-acre eco-friendly campus, the university has over 55+ years of academic excellence and educates students across more than 250 affiliated colleges.

To make quality higher education universally accessible, CCSU established its dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE). CCSU Online leverages an intelligent digital Learning Management System (LMS) to offer fully recognized undergraduate and postgraduate degrees. Whether you are a working professional looking to upskill or a student seeking an affordable, flexible degree from a top-ranked government university, CCSU Online delivers a standardized, industry-aligned curriculum.

Crucially, as per Regulation 22 of the UGC (2020), degrees earned through CCSU’s online and ODL modes are recognized as completely equal in academic and legal standing to conventional offline degrees for government jobs, civil service exams (UPSC/SSC), higher studies, and global corporate careers.

Advantages of CCSU Online and Learning Methodology

Key academic pillars, digital delivery tools, and specialized learner benefits:

Track 1: 100% Pure Online Mode

Digitally enabled learning with interactive live webinars, recorded video archives, e-books, online assignment submissions, and virtual student support. Perfect for working professionals requiring 24/7 anytime, anywhere study access.

Track 2: Open and Distance Learning (ODL)

Self-paced study model providing comprehensive printed self-learning materials (SLM) dispatched to students, alongside digital access. Supported by weekend academic counseling sessions at designated university study centers.

Track 3: Degree with Internship (Unique USP)

An innovative, high-employability program structure combining online academic coursework with structured on-site corporate internships. Students graduate with both an accredited degree and verified industry work experience.

Intelligent Learning Management System (LMS)

Cloud-based virtual platform offering seamless digital access to live lecture links, study modules, e-library resources, fee receipts, and direct academic query resolution with university professors.

Continuous Evaluation and Quizzes

Semester-based continuous assessments featuring online quizzes, chapter tests, 30% internal continuous assessments (CIA), and proctored term-end examinations designed for objective, transparent grading.

Dedicated Learner Support and Mentorship

Personalized academic guidance from experienced faculty mentors, prompt technical helpdesks, administrative assistance, and career readiness sessions to ensure smooth learning from admission to graduation.

Academic Counselor Verdict: Is CCSU Online Worth It?

Senior Academic Counselor Perspective on CCSU Online

Chaudhary Charan Singh University Online (CCSU CDOE) is one of the most reliable and affordable options for students seeking a genuine government university degree. Backed by a NAAC A++ rating (3.66 CGPA) and formal UGC-DEB entitlements, a degree from CCSU Online protects your career credentials across government jobs (UPSC, UPPSC, SSC, Banking) and foreign evaluations (WES).

What truly sets CCSU apart in 2027 is its 'Degree with Internship' track, solving the single biggest drawback of distance learning—lack of practical workplace experience. With subsidized state university tuition, it provides outstanding ROI compared to private online universities for similar curriculum.

Key Advantages (Pros)

  • Government University Prestige: 55+ year heritage with top-tier NAAC A++ rating.
  • UGC Regulation 22 Validated: 100% equivalent to regular on-campus degree.
  • Degree with Internship: Combines online learning with real-world corporate internship.
  • High Affordability and ROI: Low fees with zero-cost EMI payment facility.
  • Global Credential Acceptance: Recognized for WES evaluation and foreign PR/work permits.

Points to Consider (Cons)

  • Independent Placement Effort: Corporate placement assistance is provided, but high-tier salary packages require self-driven skill preparation.
  • Self-Discipline Needed: As with all online formats, success depends on consistent self-study on the LMS.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is CCSU Online degree valid and equivalent to a regular degree? ▾
Yes, absolutely. As per Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, degrees acquired through ODL/Online mode from recognized universities like CCSU are treated as fully equivalent to degrees offered through traditional conventional mode for employment, government examinations (UPSC, SSC, Banking), and higher education.
Is Chaudhary Charan Singh University UGC-DEB approved for online programs? ▾
Yes. CCSU's Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) is formally entitled and approved by the UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) and AICTE to deliver online and ODL undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
What are the 3 learning tracks offered by CCSU Online? ▾
CCSU Online offers three distinct educational formats: (1) 100% Pure Online Mode with digital LMS and interactive live sessions, (2) Open and Distance Learning (ODL) with printed self-learning material, and (3) Degree with Internship which blends online academics with hands-on on-site corporate internships.
What are the popular online courses offered by CCSU CDOE? ▾
CCSU Online offers Online MBA (15 specializations), Online MCA (3 specializations), M.Com (5 specializations), MA (6 specializations), Online BBA, and Online BA with optional industry internship tracks.
What financial aid and installment options are available for CCSU Online programs? ▾
As a premier state public university, CCSU Online programs operate under subsidized public tuition guidelines, with semester installment facilities and state scholarship options for eligible applicants.
Can working professionals apply for CCSU Online courses? ▾
Yes. CCSU Online is specifically structured for working professionals, self-paced learners, and remote students, offering 24/7 LMS access, recorded video archives, and weekend interaction sessions.

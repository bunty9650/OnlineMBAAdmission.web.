About Shoolini University Online (Shoolini CDOE)

Shoolini University of Biotechnology and Management Sciences, nestled in the Himalayan foothills of Solan, Himachal Pradesh, is an internationally acclaimed research institution established in 2009. Recognized as India's #1 Private University by the prestigious QS World University Rankings and ranked among the World's Top 200 in Times Higher Education (THE) Global Impact, Shoolini has emerged as a beacon of academic rigor and research innovation.

Through its dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), Shoolini University delivers fully entitled online degree programs recognized by the University Grants Commission - Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) and AICTE. Shoolini Online integrates Stanford-inspired pedagogical frameworks, mentorship by Fortune 500 CXOs, hands-on business simulations, and advanced digital LMS platforms to empower learners with executive competence.

In accordance with Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, online degrees awarded by Shoolini University hold complete equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees for corporate recruitments, central/state civil services, and international postgraduate study.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Shoolini University Online MBA valid for corporate and government jobs? +

Yes. In full accordance with UGC Regulation 22 (2020), online degrees granted by UGC-DEB entitled universities like Shoolini are treated with equal validity to on-campus degrees for all corporate, MNC, and government recruitments.

What are the eligibility requirements for Shoolini Online MBA in 2027? +

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Working experience is beneficial but not mandatory.

Are CAT or MAT scores mandatory for admission? +

No. Admission is based on academic merit and profile verification by the university admissions desk. CAT, MAT, or CMAT scores are not required.

How does the Pay After Placement (PAP) scheme operate? +

Eligible students pay a nominal semester fee during the two years of academic study and settle the placement balance fee only upon receiving an employment offer through the university placement network.

How are examinations conducted for remote learners? +

All term-end examinations are conducted 100% online through an AI-proctored remote examination system, allowing students to take tests from home using a laptop or desktop with camera and microphone.

1. Institutional Overview and Statutory Accreditations

Alliance University is established under the Karnataka State Act No. 34 of 2010 and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) as South India's first private university. With approvals from the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Bar Council of India (BCI), and scientific recognition as a SIRO institution by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR), Alliance University maintains benchmark academic standards across business administration and multidisciplinary research.

Ranked 71st among management institutions in India by NIRF and placing within the top 1% of universities in South Asia by QS World University Rankings, Alliance University holds the prestigious NAAC Grade 'A+' accreditation and a QS I-GAUGE Diamond institutional rating. Furthermore, Alliance School of Business is an active institutional member of AACSB International (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business, USA), reaffirming its curriculum alignment with international standards of management education.

Global Recognitions

Member of AACSB USA, IACBE, and QS I-GAUGE Diamond rated for academic excellence, governance, and faculty quality.

Regulatory Entitlements

Fully entitled by UGC-DEB and approved by AICTE to grant 2-year Master of Business Administration degrees in digital mode.

6. Learning Management System (LMS), Pedagogy and Examination Format

Alliance Online learning is hosted on a state-of-the-art LMS built in strict compliance with the University Grants Commission's four-quadrant e-learning architecture:

Quadrant 1 and 2: Video and Content

High-definition recorded video lectures, live weekend masterclasses by Professors of Practice, downloadable e-books, case repositories, and reading materials.

Quadrant 3 and 4: Forums and Assessment

Interactive discussion boards, peer learning forums, continuous knowledge checks, weekly quizzes, and automated practice test banks.

Examination and Evaluation Policy (70:30 Ratio)

The academic evaluation follows a structured 70:30 ratio between summative and formative assessments:

  • Semester-End Examination (SEE) - 70% Weightage: Conducted online in a computer-based, remotely proctored format. Candidates take exams from home using a webcam-enabled computer with flexible scheduling windows. The exam paper features both descriptive analytical questions and conceptual multiple-choice items.
  • During-Semester Assessment (DSA) - 30% Weightage: Formative continuous evaluation consisting of guided corporate assignments, analytical quizzes, and LMS course activities.
  • Passing Criterion: Students must maintain consistent participation and achieve a minimum of 50% marks in both the DSA and SEE components to pass each subject.

7. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission Process 2027

Admissions for the 2027 academic session follow a seamless merit-based digital evaluation without requiring entrance examinations like CAT, XAT, or MAT:

Academic Eligibility Criteria

• Candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration from any UGC-recognized university.
• Minimum aggregate score of 50% marks in graduation (45% for reserved category SC/ST candidates).
• Working professionals, entrepreneurs, and fresh graduates from all academic disciplines (Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering) are eligible to apply.

Step-by-Step 4-Stage Digital Admission Process

  1. Step 1 - Online Application: Complete the digital application form on the official portal with basic personal and educational information.
  2. Step 2 - Document Upload: Upload scanned self-attested copies of 10th, 12th, and graduation mark sheets, degree certificate, Aadhaar card, and passport-size photograph.
  3. Step 3 - Fee Payment: Pay the one-time registration fee of ₹5,000 and select your preferred semester or EMI payment schedule.
  4. Step 4 - Verification and LMS Onboarding: The university admissions committee verifies documents, generates your student enrollment ID, and grants access to the AlliO LMS portal.

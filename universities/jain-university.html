1. About JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) Online, Bengaluru

Established in 1990 in Bengaluru, Karnataka, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) has fostered an institutional legacy of over three decades in pioneering higher education and research. Conferred with NAAC Grade 'A++' accreditation and recognized with a 5-Star rating by the Karnataka State Universities Rating Framework (KSURF), JAIN University ranks among the top higher education institutions nationwide.

Through its digital wing, JAIN Online, the university delivers an expansive portfolio of UGC-entitled postgraduate management degrees designed for working professionals and ambitious graduates. JAIN Online blends traditional business acumen with high-demand domain electives and modern digital skills.

With an active community of over 50,000 learners, weekend live classes, and an AI-embedded learning ecosystem starting from Semester 1, JAIN Online prepares executives for modern business environments.

9. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

JAIN Online follows an inclusive merit-based eligibility framework:

Academic Qualification

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years duration) in any discipline from a recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST category applicants). Final semester candidates are also eligible to apply provisionally.

Direct Admission Without CAT

Admissions are conducted directly based on undergraduate academic performance. Scores from CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT are not compulsory.

14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Jain Online MBA

What is the fee structure and installment plan for Jain Online MBA? ▾

The total tuition fee for standard electives ranges between ₹1,60,000 and ₹1,96,000 payable across 4 semester installments of approx. ₹40,000 to ₹49,000 per term. Advanced tracks (such as Data Science and AI or ACCA UK) range up to ₹2,98,000. Students can also avail of an official 15% upfront payment discount or opt for zero-cost monthly EMI starting at approx. ₹6,667 per month.

Is Jain University Online MBA entitled by UGC-DEB? ▾

Yes. JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) Online is entitled by the UGC to award online master's degrees. Under UGC regulations, online degrees carry complete academic and legal equivalence to regular on-campus degrees.

What is the NAAC accreditation grade of Jain University? ▾

Jain University is accredited by NAAC with Grade 'A++', holds AICTE approval, and has been awarded 5 stars by KSURF.

Can working professionals pursue Jain Online MBA alongside a full-time job? ▾

Yes. The program is specifically structured for working professionals with weekend live classes, 24/7 cloud LMS access, recorded sessions, and 100% online proctored examinations.

Are dual electives available at Jain Online MBA? ▾

Yes. In addition to specialized single electives, students can opt for high-demand dual electives such as Finance and Marketing, HR and Finance, Marketing and HR, and combinations with Business Analytics.

What is the eligibility requirement for Jain Online MBA in 2027? ▾

Candidates must hold a 3-year Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for SC/ST category). Admission is direct without mandatory CAT entrance scores.

About Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ Online)

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) was established in 2011 as a landmark institution of the legendary Manipal Education Group (MAHE), which has spearheaded higher education excellence in India for over 70 years. Located along the Jaipur-Ajmer Expressway, MUJ features a modern 122-acre campus holding top accreditations.

Under its online education division, MUJ Online delivers industry-aligned master's and bachelor's degrees. A standout feature of Manipal Online MBA is its strategic integration with Coursera, giving enrolled students complimentary access to thousands of professional certifications from Google, IBM, Meta, and leading global universities alongside their core degree.

Holding NAAC Grade A+ accreditation, UGC entitlement, AICTE approval, and WES listing, degrees from Manipal University Jaipur carry elite corporate reputation across BFSI, IT, Management Consulting, and global employment markets.

Step-by-Step Direct Admission Process (2027 Academic Cycle)

Admissions for the 2027 academic session are conducted online via direct merit evaluation without mandatory CAT/MAT scores. Follow this 5-step roadmap to complete your enrollment:

1

Application Registration

Register online on the official Manipal University Jaipur portal. Fill out your basic academic, professional, and contact details.

2

Select Specialization & Payment Preference

Choose your MBA specialization domain (Finance, Marketing, Analytics, IT & FinTech, SCM, etc.) and choose between semester installments or 0% EMI.

3

Document Upload & Verification

Upload digital copies of your 10th and 12th certificates, Bachelor degree marks sheets, government photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), and recent passport photograph.

4

Academic Eligibility Verification

The Manipal University admissions board verifies graduation marks (minimum 50% for General, 45% for Reserved categories). Provisional admission letter is issued.

5

Fee Payment & LMS Activation

Complete semester fee payment or confirm your zero-cost EMI. Your student portal credentials and free Coursera Enterprise subscription are activated instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (Manipal Online MBA 2027)

Direct answers to the most common queries asked by prospective applicants:

Is Manipal Online MBA degree identical to the on-campus degree? +

Yes. Under UGC Gazette Notification 2020 Regulation 22, an online MBA degree conferred by an entitled university carries 100% legal parity with conventional on-campus degrees. The degree certificate awarded states 'Master of Business Administration' under the seal of Manipal University Jaipur, and is fully recognized for government recruitment, PSU jobs, and international higher education.

How does the free Coursera access work for enrolled students? +

Upon enrollment, students receive complimentary enterprise credentials for Coursera for Campus. This provides free access to over 10,000+ professional courses and micro-credentials from top global institutions like Google, IBM, Meta, and AWS, allowing learners to earn domain certifications alongside their MBA.

Are examinations conducted completely online from home? +

Yes. All term-end semester examinations are conducted 100% online from the convenience of your home or office using remote AI-web proctoring software. You never have to travel to any physical test center.

What are the payment options and 0% interest EMI plans? +

Tuition can be paid in semester installments of ₹43,750 to ₹46,000 every 6 months, or through paperless 0% interest monthly EMIs starting at approximately ₹7,290 to ₹7,667 per month with leading financial partners.

Does Manipal Online require CAT, MAT, or other entrance exams? +

No. Admission is granted directly based on your undergraduate degree aggregate marks (minimum 50% for General, 45% for Reserved categories) without mandatory CAT/MAT scores.

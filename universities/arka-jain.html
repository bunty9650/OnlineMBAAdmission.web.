About ARKA JAIN University Online (AJU Online)

ARKA JAIN University (AJU), Jharkhand, carries a rich 15-year academic legacy originating from JAIN College in 2011. As the first private university of the Kolhan region and the fastest-growing private higher education institution in Jharkhand, AJU stands as a pioneering center of professional learning in Eastern India.

The university is an integral part of the renowned JAIN Group of Institutions (JGI), Bengaluru, founded by visionary educationist and Chancellor Dr. Chenraj Roychand. JGI encompasses 77+ educational institutions, over 75,000 students, and 10,000+ staff across 64 campuses nationwide. AJU is mentored directly by JAIN (Deemed-to-be-University), Bengaluru, infusing top-tier academic governance, rigorous pedagogy, and high industry relevance into its educational framework.

ARKA JAIN University achieved the prestigious distinction of being accredited with NAAC Grade 'A' in its very first cycle—becoming the first state private university in Bihar, Jharkhand, and West Bengal to reach this milestone. Through its digital division, ARKA JAIN Online, the university offers UGC-DEB approved programs structured around 24x7 cloud Learning Management Systems, live interactive faculty sessions, and real-world case simulations.

Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, degrees earned through ARKA JAIN Online hold complete statutory equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees, ensuring seamless acceptance for central and state government examinations, public sector employment, private corporate leadership, and international doctoral studies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is ARKA JAIN University Online MBA degree recognized and UGC-approved? ▾
Yes. ARKA JAIN University offers UGC-DEB approved online degree programs through ARKA JAIN Online. As per UGC Regulation 22 (2020), online degrees awarded by UGC-entitled institutions are treated as fully equivalent in legal and academic validity to conventional on-campus degrees.
What is the NAAC accreditation and institutional ranking of ARKA JAIN University? ▾
ARKA JAIN University is accredited with NAAC Grade 'A' in its very first cycle, becoming the first state private university in Bihar, Jharkhand, and West Bengal to achieve this recognition. In The Week - Hansa Research Survey 2025, it ranked #38 Best Private University in India, and in India Today - MDRA 2024 it ranked #52 Best Private University in India.
What are the eligibility requirements for ARKA JAIN Online MBA? ▾
Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree of minimum 3 years duration with at least 50% marks (45% marks for candidates belonging to reserved categories). Candidates in their final semester of graduation are also eligible to apply.
What specializations are available in ARKA JAIN Online MBA? ▾
Learners can choose single or dual specializations from seven industry-aligned domains: Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, Data Analytics and AI, Operations and Supply Chain Management, Project Management, and Health Care Management.
How are examinations conducted for ARKA JAIN Online programs? ▾
Examinations for online programs are conducted in Online Proctored mode. Evaluation follows a 30% Continuous Internal Assessment (assignments, projects, activities) and 70% End-Term Examination structure held twice a year across odd and even semesters.
Can working professionals pursue ARKA JAIN Online MBA? ▾
Yes. The program is specifically structured for working professionals with 24x7 cloud LMS access, live interactive sessions, recorded lectures, downloadable e-books, and case-study-driven pedagogy to allow learners to upgrade their qualifications without career breaks.

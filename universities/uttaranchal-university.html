1. About Uttaranchal University Online (U-CODE)

Established in 2013 under the Uttaranchal University Act and recognized under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act 1956, Uttaranchal University is situated in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. The university established the Uttaranchal College of Online and Distance Education (U-CODE) to extend premier higher education to working professionals and learners across India and abroad.

Accredited by NAAC with prestigious Grade 'A+' status and recognized by AICTE, AIU, and WES, Uttaranchal University combines rigorous academic research with practical industry immersion. Its Online MBA program provides flexible live and recorded instruction tailored for modern career advancement.

NAAC A+ Rating

Top-tier national accreditation reflecting academic quality, pedagogy, and research-intensive faculty.

1-on-1 Mentorship

Every student is paired with a dedicated faculty mentor to navigate coursework and career planning.

8 Industry Tracks

Specialized electives spanning Analytics, Logistics, IT, Digital Marketing, Finance, and International Business.

9. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to Uttaranchal University Online MBA follows clear academic merit norms:

Qualifying Bachelor's Degree

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree of minimum three years duration in any discipline from a recognized university or institution.

Minimum Aggregate Marks

A minimum of 50% marks in aggregate for General category applicants, and 45% marks in case of candidates belonging to Reserved categories.

No Entrance Exam Required

Admissions are conducted on undergraduate merit. Scores from CAT, MAT, XAT, or CMAT are not compulsory.

Working Executives

Working professionals with 2 years of work experience are also encouraged to apply for enhanced executive cohort learning.

14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - UU Online MBA

What is the fee structure and installment plan for UU Online MBA? ▾

The standard fee is ₹35,000 per semester (total ₹1,40,000). With up to 30% early-bird scholarship applied, the net fee is ₹24,500 per semester, ₹47,000 annually, or ₹94,000 for one-time upfront payment. No-cost EMI options start at ₹3,956 per month.

Is Uttaranchal University Online MBA recognized by UGC-DEB? ▾

Yes. Uttaranchal University holds UGC entitlement for its online MBA program. The degree carries full legal equivalence to traditional on-campus degrees under UGC regulations.

What are the class timings and weekly commitment? ▾

The program follows a hybrid live and recorded model requiring approximately 8 to 10 hours per week of study time, making it convenient to balance with full-time professional commitments.

How many specializations are available at UU Online? ▾

There are 8 verified specializations: Marketing Management, Financial Management, HRM, Business Analytics, Information Technology, Digital Marketing, International Business, and Logistics and Supply Chain Management.

What is the eligibility requirement for 2027 admission? ▾

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years duration) with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category). Direct admission is available without entrance exam scores.

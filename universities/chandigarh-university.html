1. About Chandigarh University (Centre for Distance and Online Education)

Chandigarh University (CU) is a leading higher education institution situated in Punjab near Chandigarh. Operating across a sprawling 150-acre smart campus with world-class academic infrastructure, the university caters to students from 28 Indian states, 8 union territories, and over 65 countries worldwide. Through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CU-CDOE), Chandigarh University extends its distinguished academic pedigree globally, offering flexible, interactive, and career-oriented online degree programs.

CU Online has established international partnerships with more than 502 top-ranked universities across 95 countries, fostering an interdisciplinary, globally-benchmarked educational ecosystem. Guided by Hon'ble Chancellor S. Satnam Singh Sandhu, Pro-Chancellor Prof. Himani Sood, and Vice-Chancellor Prof. (Dr.) Manpreet Singh Manna, Chandigarh University Online bridges theoretical business knowledge with real-world corporate competencies to prepare ambitious professionals for executive leadership in the modern global economy.

Global Footprint

Active international academic partnerships with 502+ institutions across 95 countries, enriching courses with global business case studies.

150-Acre Smart Campus

Backed by physical infrastructure, advanced research labs, and expansive studio setups for high-definition live lecture broadcasting.

Diverse Student Community

Learners hailing from all 28 Indian states, 8 union territories, and 65 international nations creating a powerful executive alumni network.

4. Triple Industry Credentials and Global Corporate Tie-ups

A defining benchmark of the Chandigarh University Online MBA is its direct integration of credentials and coursework from premier global industry partners:

PwC Co-Created and Delivered Core Curriculum

Two pivotal management courses in the curriculum—Financial Reporting and Analysis (Semester 1) and Financial Management (Semester 2)—are co-created and delivered directly by subject-matter experts from PwC. Learners receive an official co-branded PwC certificate upon completion, providing unmatched real-world corporate financial depth.

PMI CAPM Certification Preparation

Learners receive complete preparation for the globally renowned Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) credential from the Project Management Institute (PMI). Includes 23 hours of ATP instructor-led training, 23 requisite PDUs, and a complimentary 1-year PMI Student Membership.

Harvard Business Publishing Education (HBPE)

Master 10 critical executive leadership modules powered by Harvard ManageMentor: Innovation and Creativity, Strategy Planning and Execution, Business Plan Development, Performance Measurement, Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging, Decision Making, Ethics At Work, Presentation Skills, Team Management, and Digital Intelligence.

Harvard Business School Online (MBA X Track)

CU offers an exclusive collaborative MBA X program with Harvard Business School Online featuring the prestigious 12-week Harvard CORe (Credential of Readiness) covering Business Analytics, Economics for Managers, and Financial Accounting with flexible credit transfers.

13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Chandigarh University Online MBA

Is Chandigarh University Online MBA degree recognized and UGC-DEB approved? ▾

Yes. Chandigarh University is entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) to offer online degree programs through the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CU-CDOE). Under UGC Regulation 22 (2020), online degrees awarded by UGC-entitled institutions hold complete academic and legal equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees.

What are the NAAC accreditation and global rankings of Chandigarh University? ▾

Chandigarh University is accredited with NAAC Grade 'A+' with a 3.53 CGPA. In the QS Asia University Rankings 2025, Chandigarh University ranked #1 among all Private Universities in India. It is also ranked 20th in India by NIRF 2024 (Ministry of Education) and ranked #691-700 in the QS World University Rankings.

What triple industry certifications are integrated into CU Online MBA? ▾

The CU Online MBA integrates triple industry credentials: (1) PwC co-created and taught core courses in Financial Reporting and Analysis and Financial Management with PwC certification; (2) PMI CAPM exam preparation with 23 hours ATP training, 23 PDUs, and a 1-year PMI student membership; and (3) Harvard Business Publishing Education access to 10 Harvard ManageMentor leadership modules.

How many credits and specializations are in Chandigarh University Online MBA? ▾

The program comprises a 92-credit curriculum spread over 4 semesters (Semester 1: 20 credits, Semester 2: 20 credits, Semester 3: 28 credits, Semester 4: 24 credits). Learners can choose from 17 specialized tracks including Marketing, Finance, HR, Business Analytics, IT, Operations, Digital Marketing, and dual specializations.

How are online examinations and degree verifications conducted? ▾

Examinations are conducted online in proctored mode with on-demand scheduling flexibility and a re-exam facility in semester-end exams. Degrees are deposited directly into the Government of India DigiLocker system for instant verification and worldwide credential mobility.

What is the eligibility criteria for Chandigarh University Online MBA in 2027? ▾

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution. Graduates of professional programs such as CA, ICWA, and CS are also eligible. Admission is direct on merit without mandatory entrance examination scores.

About Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU)

The Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) was established by an Act of Parliament in 1985 with a statutory mandate to democratise higher education. As a pioneer in open and distance education, IGNOU was founded to achieve four core national objectives:

  • Democratising higher education by taking it to the doorsteps of the learners.
  • Providing access to high quality education to all those who seek it irrespective of age, region, religion, and gender.
  • Offering need-based academic programmes by giving professional and vocational orientation to the courses.
  • Promoting and developing distance education in India.

Over the decades, IGNOU has emerged as the largest Open University in the world. In 2010, UNESCO declared IGNOU as the largest institution of higher learning in the world. It holds the historic distinction of being the first open university in the country to have been accredited with the highest NAAC A++ Grade.

Headquartered in New Delhi, the university operates through a network of 21 specialized Schools of Studies, including the renowned School of Management Studies (SOMS). Academic programmes are backed nationwide by 67 Regional Centres and Recognised Regional Centres and a widespread student support network of Learner Support Centres across India.

Modular Approach and Credit System Architecture

Modular Architecture and Pathways

Programmes follow a modular approach in compliance with NEP 2020. Under this approach, each course is allocated a specific credit weightage. Modularity is implemented through two distinct pathways:

  • Exit Options: Students not wishing to complete an entire degree can exit at designated credit points and earn a Diploma or PG Diploma to meet immediate career needs.
  • Lateral Entry: Learners completing a Diploma or PG Diploma are allowed lateral entry into degree programmes to upgrade qualifications conveniently.

The Credit System (30 Hours / Credit)

The University follows the Credit System for most of its programmes. Each credit is equivalent to 30 hours of learner study, encompassing:

  • Reading and comprehending printed study material and e-books
  • Listening to audio programmes and watching instructional videos
  • Attending counselling sessions and satellite teleconferences
  • Writing assignment responses and preparing project work

Thus, a 4-credit course involves 120 hours of study and a 6-credit course involves 180 hours of study.

Nationwide Student Support Services and Infrastructure

To provide individualized support to its learners, IGNOU has established the largest network of learning support systems in the country:

67 Regional Centres and Pan-India Network

Individualized support is coordinated across 67 Regional Centres and Recognised Regional Centres. At the Learner Support Centres (LSCs), learners interact with Academic Counselors and peer students, refer to library books, utilize audio-video facilities, and interact with Coordinators on administrative and academic matters.

Specialized Support Centres and TELS

Support services are extended through Work Centres, Programme Learner Support Centres, Skill Development Centres, and Special Learner Support Centres. Online programmes are delivered on the University LMS Platform. For smaller cohorts, Technology Enabled Learner Support (TELS) is provided through Gyan Darshan, Gyan Vani, Swayam Prabha, and web-based systems.

Programme Delivery and Multimedia Learning Methodology

The methodology of instruction in IGNOU is different from that of conventional universities. The Open University system is learner-oriented, with the learner being an active participant in the pedagogical process. Instruction is imparted through distance and online education methodologies using a multimedia approach:

Instructional Component Delivery Format and Pedagogical Approach
Self-Instructional Printed Material and E-Books Study material written in self-instructional style provided as single print books, e-books, or separate printed blocks (average 1 block per credit; each block comprises 3 to 5 units).
Audio-Visual Learning Aids 25-30 minute audio and video programmes produced by the university; accessible via eGyanKosh digital repository, Gyan Darshan, Swayam Prabha, and Gyan Vani FM.
Academic Counselling Sessions Organized by Learner Support Centres outside regular working hours; Web Enabled Academic Support (WEAS) provided when cohorts are smaller.
Satellite Teleconferences Live interactive sessions conducted via satellite through interactive Gyan Darshan Channel, simultaneous webcasts, and interactive sessions.
Practicals and Project Work Practical sessions held at designated institutions with compulsory attendance; comprehensive project guide booklets provided with study materials.
Term-End Examinations (TEE) Comprehensive evaluation combining assignments, practicals, projects, and Term-End Examinations, with results declared within 45 days.

Media and Digital Learning Ecosystem

Gyan Darshan (GD)

A 24-hour educational TV channel launched in 2000 as a joint venture of MHRD, I&B Ministry, Prasar Bharati, and IGNOU as nodal agency. Features regular broadcasts from Electronic Media Production Centre (EMPC) studios, including two hours of daily live interactive sessions, induction programmes, and convocations. Part of Swayam Prabha on MHRD Channel no. 25.

Gyan Vani (GV) FM Radio

Educational FM Radio network conceived in 2001 operating across Indian cities with a range of 60 km covering urban and adjoining rural areas. Features Interactive Radio Counseling (IRC) with live phone-in programmes in English, Hindi, and regional languages.

Swayam Prabha Project

Ministry of Education initiative providing 34 24x7 high quality educational TV channels through DTH on GSAT 15 satellite. IGNOU serves as National Coordinator for Channels 17 to 20, broadcasting recorded and live telecasts.

Gyandhara Audio Service

Internet audio counseling service streaming live discussions by teachers and experts worldwide with phone, email, and chat interactivity; relays Gyan Vani Delhi broadcasts when live sessions are off-air.

Web Enabled Academic Support (WEAS)

Single-window platform for ODL programmes providing course material in digital format, video resources, quizzes, discussion forums, calendar of activities, and virtual interaction with teachers and peers.

eGyanKosh and e-Content Mobile App

eGyanKosh is a national digital repository of higher education resources available free to learners and teachers. Complemented by the IGNOU e-Content Mobile App for smartphones and tablets.

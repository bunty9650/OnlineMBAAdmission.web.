1. Institutional Overview and Statutory Recognitions

Bharati Vidyapeeth is one of India's preeminent higher education networks, established in 1964 in Pune, Maharashtra. Conferred the status of Deemed to be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, Bharati Vidyapeeth has expanded across multiple campuses with 29 constituent institutions. The Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) is dedicated to delivering cutting-edge, flexible digital postgraduate qualifications.

Re-accredited with the prestigious NAAC Grade 'A+' and accorded Category-1 Autonomy by the UGC, Bharati Vidyapeeth CDOE holds recognition from UGC-DEB and approval from AICTE. The university consistently ranks within the top 100 higher education institutions nationwide by NIRF, providing graduates with degrees that hold unconditional legal equivalence to regular on-campus management qualifications.

Category-1 Autonomy

Recognized by UGC for outstanding academic governance, allowing BVDU to offer high-standard online programs matching international benchmarks.

Dual Specialization Freedom

Students can select dual specialization combinations across 14 commercial and technical domains during Semester 3 to build competitive career skills.

6. Learning Management System (LMS), Pedagogy and Examinations

All academic sessions, live webinars, and study resources are accessed through the official Bharati Vidyapeeth CDOE LMS portal:

Digital Study Resources

Students receive e-books, live interactive lectures by eminent university faculty, recorded lecture archives, and curated discussion forums.

Academic Mentors

Each student is allocated a dedicated faculty mentor to guide them through the maker-checker verification, academic doubts, and summer internship.

Examination Guidelines and Passing Standards

  • Semester-End Online Examinations: Online examinations are conducted at the conclusion of each semester using web and mobile assessment interfaces.
  • Passing Percentage: Students must secure a minimum Grade Point of 5.0 (equivalent to 40% aggregate marks) in each course to qualify.
  • Maximum Course Validity: The standard program duration is 24 months (2 years). Students are permitted up to four academic years from the date of admission to clear all course requirements.

7. Eligibility Criteria and Digital Admission Flow 2027

Admissions for the 2027 batch are conducted online based on graduation merit:

Academic Eligibility Criteria

• Admission is open to any graduate (10+2+3 pattern) from any recognized university.
• The candidate must have secured at least 50% marks in aggregate at graduation (45% for SC/ST candidates).
• Candidates appearing in the final year of their Bachelor's degree may also apply provisionally.

Digital Admission and Maker-Checker Flow

  1. Online Registration: Register student account on the official CDOE portal and complete email validation.
  2. Application and Document Submission: Fill in personal, educational, and program details. Upload scanned copies of 10th and 12th mark sheets, graduation certificates, Aadhaar card, ABC ID (Academic Bank of Credits), and passport photograph.
  3. Fee Payment: Pay the first semester fee of ₹46,000 (including ₹2,000 registration fee) via secure net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI.
  4. Maker-Checker Document Verification: University scrutiny teams verify submitted original documents. Provisional admission is confirmed via official email.
  5. Orientation and LMS Allocation: Attend compulsory academic orientation and access course materials, live class schedules, and mentor assignments on the LMS.

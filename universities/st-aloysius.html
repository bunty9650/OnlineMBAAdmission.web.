About St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University)

St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University) was founded in 1880 by the Society of Jesus (Jesuits) atop the scenic Lighthouse Hill in Kodialbail, Mangaluru, Karnataka. With an illustrious legacy spanning 146 years, the institution has nurtured generations of national leaders, corporate innovators, scientists, and civil servants under its motto "Lucet et Ardet" (Shine to Enkindle).

In January 2024, the Ministry of Education and University Grants Commission (UGC) conferred Deemed-to-be University status upon St. Aloysius, accompanied by prestigious UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy. This milestone empowers the university to design cutting-edge, future-forward digital curricula and deliver high-impact postgraduate degree programs online.

Under its digital education arm, St. Aloysius Online offers a distinctive Online Master of Business Administration (MBA) that addresses the primary demand of today's executive job market: Practical Applied AI integration. Rather than treating AI as an isolated elective or theoretical overview, St. Aloysius weaves domain-specific Artificial Intelligence into core management specializations, supplemented by interactive simulations through its dedicated Centre of Excellence in AI & Applied Learning.

Frequently Asked Questions (St. Aloysius Online MBA 2027)

Direct answers to the most common queries asked by prospective applicants:

Is St. Aloysius Online MBA recognized by UGC and AICTE? +

Yes. St. Aloysius (Deemed to be University) is NAAC A++ accredited (CGPA 3.67/4) and was conferred Deemed-to-be University status with UGC Category-I Graded Autonomy in 2024. Its management technical programs are AICTE compliant, and degrees have full statutory equivalence to conventional on-campus master's degrees.

What is the Applied AI track and how is it taught? +

St. Aloysius integrates Applied AI directly into the core curriculum of every specialisation. Students learn how AI transforms real-world business functions—from predictive financial models to automated marketing funnels—supported by practical simulations accessible via the Centre of Excellence in AI & Applied Learning within their LMS.

Do I need to visit Mangaluru campus for classes or exams? +

No. The program is 100% online. Both live lectures and term-end semester examinations are conducted remotely from home using secure AI-proctored web software. Students are cordially invited to visit the historic Mangaluru campus for the annual convocation ceremony if they wish.

What are the total fees and installment options? +

The standard MBA tuition is ₹80,000 total (payable in 4 semester installments of ₹20,000), while the Applied AI enhanced specialization pathway is ₹1,20,000 total (₹30,000 per semester). No-cost paperless monthly EMI options start from ₹3,333 to ₹4,500 per month.

Is CAT, MAT, or any entrance exam required? +

No. Admission is granted directly based on undergraduate marks (minimum 50% for General, 45% for Reserved categories). No entrance exam or scorecard submission is required.

Is the degree valid for government jobs, UPSC, and foreign evaluation (WES)? +

Yes. Under UGC Regulation 22, online master degrees awarded by recognized Deemed-to-be universities carry full legal parity with traditional on-campus degrees. They are 100% valid for UPSC, SSC, banking, state PSC recruitment, and accepted by WES for higher education or immigration abroad.

About Jamia Hamdard University Online (Jamia Hamdard CDOE)

Jamia Hamdard is an internationally acclaimed government-aided deemed-to-be university located in Hamdard Nagar, New Delhi. Established in 1989 through the visionary philanthropy of Hakeem Abdul Hameed, Jamia Hamdard represents the unification of distinguished educational institutions dedicated to medical, pharmaceutical, management, and scientific research. The university was awarded Deemed-to-be University status by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, and has continuously excelled across national and global rankings.

Through its dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), founded to democratize premier education, Jamia Hamdard delivers fully entitled online postgraduate management programs. Designed for working professionals, corporate executives, and graduates across India and abroad, the Online MBA blends rigorous academic coursework with self-paced digital flexibility. Operating under the institutional philosophy "Think Beyond. Reach Further. Serve Greater," Jamia Hamdard empowers learners with strategic management acumen and global corporate competencies.

In accordance with Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, online degrees awarded by Jamia Hamdard hold complete equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees for corporate recruitments, central/state civil services, and international postgraduate study.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Clear answers to key questions regarding Jamia Hamdard Online degrees, validity, admissions, and careers:

Is Jamia Hamdard Online degree valid and equivalent to a regular degree? +

Yes, absolutely. Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, online degrees awarded by UGC-entitled universities like Jamia Hamdard hold 100% legal equivalence to conventional full-time degrees for government employment, private sector promotions, and international higher education.

Is CAT, MAT, or CMAT mandatory for admission to Jamia Hamdard Online MBA? +

No. Under UGC-DEB distance and online education guidelines, national entrance tests like CAT, MAT, or CMAT are not mandatory. Admission is granted directly on the basis of academic merit in your qualifying Bachelor's degree (minimum 50% aggregate marks, 45% for reserved categories).

How are examinations conducted in Jamia Hamdard Online MBA? +

Examinations are conducted 100% online through an AI-enabled remote proctoring platform. Students can take their semester end exams conveniently from home or office using a computer with a webcam and microphone.

What are the installment payment options available? +

Students can pay their fees in annual installments (Year 1: Rs 52,000, Year 2: Rs 51,500) or opt for semester-wise payments and no-cost monthly EMI facilities through banking partners.

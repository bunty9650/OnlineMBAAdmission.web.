About DU SOL MBA (School of Open Learning, University of Delhi)

The School of Open Learning (SOL) at the University of Delhi (DU) has embraced online and distance learning as part of its foundational commitment to providing excellent higher education. Ranked among the Top 10 Universities in India, Delhi University offers an Online Master of Business Administration (MBA) program that blends flexibility, convenience, and academic achievement.

This 2-year management program meets the changing demands of working professionals and aspiring students, allowing them to pursue higher career aspirations without jeopardizing their current work obligations. What makes the DU SOL Online MBA exceptional is that its curriculum and pedagogical framework are curated by professors from the iconic Faculty of Management Studies (FMS) Delhi, upholding Delhi University's strong academic standards.

A degree obtained through the ODL mode from the University of Delhi is equivalent to a traditional on-campus degree. It holds equal recognition across industry, government departments, and higher educational institutions worldwide, equipping learners for managerial positions, entrepreneurship, consultancy, and leadership roles.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is DU SOL Online MBA degree equivalent to a regular Delhi University degree? ▾
Yes. A degree obtained through DU SOL ODL mode is equivalent to a traditional on-campus degree, accepted by top recruiters worldwide in both government and corporate sectors.
What is the duration of the DU SOL Online MBA program? ▾
The program usually lasts 2 years, with flexible schedules allowing working professionals to balance career and study commitments.
What are the eligibility requirements for DU SOL Online MBA? ▾
Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution with minimum qualifying percentage or comparable CGPA stipulated by DU SOL.
What are the admission cycles available each year at DU SOL? ▾
In compliance with UGC guidelines, DU SOL offers admissions in two academic sessions every year: the 1st Batch in July/August and the 2nd Batch in January/February.
How are examinations conducted for DU SOL Online MBA? ▾
Examinations feature continuous evaluation and computer-based end-of-term tests attended at designated testing centres, containing both objective and subjective questions.
Does DU SOL provide placement assistance for Online MBA students? ▾
Yes. DU SOL offers placement aid with over 300+ corporate hiring partners (including Tata Steel, PwC, TCS, Accenture, Infosys, and HDFC Bank), reporting a 50% average salary hike and 3X increase in interview opportunities.

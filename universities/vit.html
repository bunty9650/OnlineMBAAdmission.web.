About Vellore Institute of Technology Online (VIT Online)

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is one of India's most prestigious and highest-ranked multidisciplinary higher education institutions. Founded in 1984 as the Vellore Engineering College by visionary educationist Dr. G. Viswanathan and conferred Deemed-to-be University status by the Ministry of Education, Government of India in 2001, VIT has consistently set benchmarks for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and industry-collaborative education.

With major campuses spanning Vellore, Chennai, Amaravati (AP), and Bhopal, VIT has built a formidable global reputation, recognized as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) and consistently featured among the top universities in national and international rankings. Through the Directorate of Online Education (VIT Online), the institution extends its world-class management education to ambitious working professionals, corporate managers, and graduates across the globe.

The VIT Online MBA program combines rigorous academic foundations with flexible digital delivery. In full accordance with Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, degrees awarded by VIT through online mode carry complete legal equivalence to conventional on-campus master's degrees for recruitment in central and state governments, public sector undertakings, multinational corporations, and global doctoral admissions.

Examinations and Evaluation Architecture

VIT Online follows a rigorous, credible examination methodology compliant with statutory UGC-DEB norms:

Evaluation Component Weightage Format and Details
Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) 30% Weightage Continuous evaluation via the learning portal: weekly formative quizzes, analytical case studies, and business problem assignments.
Term-End Semester Examination 70% Weightage Final assessment conducted in designated offline proctored examination test centres nationwide to maintain academic integrity and degree value.
Passing Standard Minimum 50% Marks Participants must secure an overall score of at least 50% in each course and subject to qualify for credit conferral.

Step-by-Step Admission Process 2027 (January 2027 Batch)

Securing admission to the VIT Online MBA involves a straightforward 4-step digital workflow:

Step 1: Lead Registration and Application

Complete the online application form and pay the non-refundable application processing fee of ₹1,200/-.

Step 2: Document Verification

Upload government ID, 10th and 12th certificates, and undergraduate degree marksheets (minimum 50% aggregate, 45% for SC/ST).

Step 3: Registration Advance and Seat Confirmation

Pay the initial registration advance of ₹10,000/- to confirm your seat, which is adjusted against your Semester 1 tuition fee.

Step 4: Batch Selection and LMS Onboarding

Select your preferred batch schedule (weekday or weekend batch), receive your student portal credentials, and begin orientation.

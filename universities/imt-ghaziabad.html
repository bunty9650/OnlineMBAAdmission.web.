About IMT Ghaziabad (Centre for Distance Learning - CDL)

Institute of Management Technology (IMT) Ghaziabad, established in 1980 in Delhi NCR, is universally recognized as one of India's elite Top 10 Business Schools. Over more than four decades, IMT has produced prominent corporate leaders, managing directors, and innovative entrepreneurs who shape Indian and global business.

Through its dedicated Centre for Distance Learning (IMT CDL), the institution delivers its premier Two-Year Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) designed specifically for working executives, managers, and entrepreneurs. The program replicates the academic rigor of IMT's on-campus MBA through Harvard case studies, interactive business simulations, and live weekend sessions led by renowned B-school faculty.

Holding full AICTE approval, an Executive PGDM from IMT CDL is recognized across top management consultancies, investment banks, technology giants, and Fortune 500 corporations as a benchmark of managerial competence.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is IMT Ghaziabad CDL PGDM approved by AICTE? ▾
Yes. The Two-Year Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) offered by IMT CDL is fully approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
What is the fee for IMT Ghaziabad Online PGDM? ▾
The total program fee is ₹1,60,000 payable across 4 semesters at ₹40,000 per semester, or via a zero-cost monthly EMI of ₹6,667 per month.
How are examinations conducted for IMT CDL? ▾
Examinations are conducted 100% online through secure web-proctoring technology, allowing you to take exams from home or office.
Do I need to take CAT or XAT for IMT Ghaziabad Online PGDM? ▾
No entrance exam is mandatory. Admissions are based on graduation academic performance (minimum 50% marks) and work profile evaluation.
What is the difference between IMT Ghaziabad full-time and CDL PGDM? ▾
The curriculum and case-study pedagogy are benchmarked to the same high standards, but the CDL format offers flexible weekend live classes and online assessments designed for working professionals.
Does IMT CDL use Harvard Business School case studies? ▾
Yes. IMT CDL incorporates licensed Harvard Business Publishing case studies and business simulations directly into its curriculum.

1. About Galgotias University Online (Galgotias CDOE)

Sponsored by Smt. Shakuntala Educational and Welfare Society, Galgotias University is recognized among India's leading institutions for academic excellence and innovation. In its first NAAC accreditation cycle, the university secured Grade 'A+' with a CGPA of 3.37 out of 4.

The Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) at Galgotias University is committed to delivering accessible, industry-relevant higher education tailored for working executives. Situated across a sprawling 52-acre campus in Greater Noida, UP, Galgotias University brings a 30+ year academic heritage, over 89,000 learners, and 500+ corporate recruitment partnerships to its online programs.

Dual-Major Exposure

Opportunity to pursue two major specialisations, enhancing versatility and cross-functional managerial competency.

Outcome-Based Education

Curriculum structured on OBE principles with live lectures, case-based assessments, and industry masterclasses.

Research-Oriented Capstone

Inclusion of a mandatory Industry Research Capstone to develop deep analytical problem-solving skills.

6. Eligibility Criteria and Direct Admission 2027

Admission to the Online MBA at Galgotias University follows UGC-DEB prescribed merit norms:

Undergraduate Degree Requirement

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Final semester candidates can also apply provisionally.

Direct Merit-Based Admission

Admissions are conducted on graduation academic merit without entrance exam hurdles like CAT, MAT, or CUET, ensuring a streamlined process for working adults.

7. Step-by-Step Online Admission Procedure 2027

1
Online Registration: Register on the official portal with basic contact details and email verification.
2
Application Form and ABC ID: Complete the digital application form, generate your ABC ID and UGC DEB ID via Digilocker.
3
Fee Payment: Pay the application and initial tuition fee online via the secure university payment gateway.
4
Admission Confirmation and LMS: Receive your enrollment confirmation and access your student learning management portal for live interactive sessions.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Galgotias University Online MBA UGC entitled? ▾

Yes. Galgotias University holds NAAC Grade A+ accreditation and full UGC-DEB entitlement. The online degree holds complete statutory equality with campus master degrees.

Can I pursue dual specializations at Galgotias Online? ▾

Yes. The program is structured as a dual-major degree, allowing learners to select two distinct functional tracks during Semesters 3 and 4.

How are examinations administered? ▾

Examinations are conducted 100% online through digital proctored assessment platforms without physical attendance requirements.

About Dayananda Sagar University Online (DSU CDOE)

Dayananda Sagar University (DSU) carries a celebrated academic heritage rooted in Bengaluru, India's primary innovation and technology capital. Founded through the visionary leadership of late Sri R. Dayananda Sagar in the 1960s, the Dayananda Sagar Institutions have delivered premier education across healthcare, engineering, management, and basic sciences for over seven decades. The university was formally established under the Karnataka Act No. 34 of 2014, operating as a multi-disciplinary state private university committed to research, professional training, and global academic standards.

Under its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), DSU Online delivers UGC-entitled degree programs designed for working executives, business founders, and modern graduate learners. The Online MBA curriculum combines the rigorous academic standards of Dayananda Sagar University's on-campus School of Commerce and Management Studies with cutting-edge asynchronous digital pedagogy. Learners acquire strategic business acumen, managerial problem-solving expertise, and industry-demanded tools without stepping away from full-time careers or professional obligations.

In accordance with Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, online degrees awarded by Dayananda Sagar University hold complete equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees for corporate recruitments, central/state civil services, and international postgraduate study.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Clear answers to key questions regarding DSU Online degrees, validity, admissions, and careers:

Is Dayananda Sagar University Online degree valid and equivalent to a regular degree? +

Yes, absolutely. Under Regulation 22 of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, online degrees awarded by UGC-entitled universities like DSU hold 100% legal equivalence to conventional full-time degrees for government employment, private sector promotions, and international higher education.

Is CAT, MAT, or CMAT mandatory for admission to DSU Online MBA? +

No. Under UGC-DEB distance and online education guidelines, national entrance tests like CAT, MAT, or CMAT are not mandatory. Admission is granted directly on the basis of academic merit in your qualifying Bachelor's degree (minimum 50% aggregate marks, 45% for reserved categories).

How are term-end examinations conducted in DSU Online? +

Examinations are conducted 100% online through an AI-enabled remote proctoring platform. Students can take their semester end exams conveniently from home or office using a computer with a webcam and microphone.

Can I pursue a dual specialization at DSU Online? +

Yes. DSU Online's curriculum architecture allows students to choose a primary major domain and a complementary minor domain across functional areas such as Marketing, Finance, HR, IT, Analytics, or AI.

1. About Noida International University (NIU)

Noida International University (NIU) is a premier multidisciplinary university established in 2010 by Act No. 27 of 2010 of the Uttar Pradesh State Legislature. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) and empowered to award degrees under Section 22 of the UGC Act 1956. Situated across an expansive 75-acre campus on the Yamuna Expressway in Greater Noida, NIU has emerged over 14 years of academic excellence as a leading educational hub in Delhi NCR.

NIU is accredited with NAAC Grade 'A+' with an impressive cumulative score of 3.44 out of 4.0—the highest score achieved by any private university in Cycle 1 of accreditation. The university houses 12 academic schools and the prestigious Noida International Institute of Medical Sciences (NIIMS), which operates a 750+ bedded super-speciality medical hospital.

In October 2024, the university launched its dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) to democratize quality higher education. Rooted in NIU's institutional legacy with 50,000+ alumni globally, NIU CDOE currently serves a vibrant community of over 10,000 online learners across India and 64+ nations worldwide. Under the visionary stewardship of Chairman Dr. Devesh Kumar Singh, Chancellor Dr. Vikram Singh, and Vice Chancellor Prof (Dr.) Uma Bhardwaj, NIU focuses on the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) pillars of affordability, accessibility, quality, equity, and accountability.

5. Examination and Evaluation System

Noida International University employs a modern, fair, and transparent evaluation architecture designed for working learners while maintaining strict academic integrity:

30:70 Assessment Model

30% Continuous Internal Assessment: Quizzes, case analysis submissions, mid-term assignments, and discussion participation.
70% End-Term Examination: Comprehensive semester-end exams evaluating theoretical mastery and practical problem-solving.

Hybrid Virtual Proctored System

Examinations are monitored through a dual proctoring setup: AI-enabled remote facial recognition and webcam tracking paired with on-campus human proctors to ensure 100% exam integrity without requiring campus travel.

Pre-Exam Preparation and Mock Demos

Before semester-end examinations, NIU conducts mock testing sessions, environment readiness tests, and demo walkthroughs so students are fully comfortable with the digital exam platform.

Absolute Grading Scale

Grading is determined on an absolute performance scale, guaranteeing transparent, objective, and unbiased evaluation across all candidate groups.

Noida International University maintains an inclusive, merit-based admission policy aligned with UGC Open and Distance Learning guidelines:

13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - NIU Online MBA

Is Noida International University Online MBA degree recognized and UGC-DEB approved? ▾

Yes. Noida International University is entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) to offer online degree programs through the Centre for Distance and Online Education (NIU CDOE). Under UGC Regulation 22 (2020), online degrees awarded by UGC-entitled institutions hold complete academic and legal equivalence to conventional on-campus degrees.

What is the NAAC accreditation grade and ranking of Noida International University? ▾

Noida International University is accredited with NAAC Grade 'A+' with a 3.44 CGPA score, which is the highest score achieved by private universities in Cycle 1 of accreditation. NIU is also a member of AIU, UNAI, and AACSB Business Education Alliance.

How many credits and specializations are offered in NIU Online MBA? ▾

The program features a 104-credit curriculum spread over 4 semesters (2 years). Learners can select from 10 verified specializations including Finance, Marketing, HR, International Business, IT, Operations, Retail, Big Data Analytics, Hospital Management, and Pharmaceuticals.

How are examinations conducted for online learners at NIU? ▾

Examinations follow a balanced 30:70 model (30% internal assignments and 70% end-term exams). Tests are administered through a hybrid virtual proctored examination system combining AI webcam monitoring and live human proctoring.

What is the eligibility criteria for Noida International University Online MBA in 2027? ▾

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Admission is direct on academic merit without mandatory entrance examination scores such as CAT, MAT, or XAT.

Can working professionals manage the study schedule while working full-time? ▾

Yes. The program is specifically built for working professionals with 24/7 cloud LMS access, recorded lecture archives, weekend live doubt clearing, and digital submission of assessments.

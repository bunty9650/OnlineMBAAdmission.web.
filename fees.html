Payment Support

How Does 0% No-Cost EMI Work for Online MBA?

You don't need to pay the entire 2-year fee upfront. Through our university financing partnerships (LiquiLoans, Propelld, Eduvanz), students can divide their semester or total fee into 10, 12, or 24 equal monthly installments with zero extra interest charges.

  • ✓ Zero interest burden (University pays the interest cost)
  • ✓ Minimal documentation (Aadhaar, PAN and 3-month bank statement)
  • ✓ Instant online loan approval within 2 to 4 hours
  • ✓ No credit card mandatory (Debit card and Net Banking options available)
